## Distinction

AI Physiology does not merely ask whether artificial systems can exhibit life-like behavior.

It asks how an AI system can be explicitly engineered as a persistent organism with organs, internal transport, metabolism, regulation, immunity, repair, lifecycle, and homeostasis.
