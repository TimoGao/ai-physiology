3. What is the smallest viable AI Cell?
4. Which internal variables qualify as AI vital signs?
5. Can artificial homeostasis improve long-horizon agent reliability?
6. Can organ-level autonomy reduce central-model dependence?
7. What should AI Blood transport, and what should remain outside it?
8. Do AI systems require separate neural, circulatory, hormonal, and immune planes?
9. What is the functional equivalent of metabolism in digital AI?
10. What constitutes toxic internal information?
11. How should memory aging and degradation be modeled?
12. What is an AI kidney, operationally?
13. Can immune memory improve resilience to repeated attack patterns?
14. What is organism identity under continuous hardware/model replacement?
15. What constitutes growth versus self-modification?
16. What constitutes reproduction?
17. What constitutes death?
18. Which functions should remain external rather than internalized?
19. How does environment shape AI morphology?
20. How should organism-level physiology connect to society-level governance?
