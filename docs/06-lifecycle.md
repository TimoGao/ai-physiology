# AI Lifecycle

AI Physiology treats lifecycle as a native system property.

## Stages
- creation
- initialization
- growth
- specialization
- stable operation
- stress
- damage
- repair
- aging
- replacement
- retirement / death

## Local death vs organism death

A defining property of resilient physiology is that local component loss does not necessarily terminate the whole organism.

Possible states:
- cell failure
- tissue degradation
- organ failure
- systemic failure
- organism death

## Open questions
- What defines continuity of identity after hardware replacement?
- Can an organ be fully replaced while the organism remains the same individual?
- When does self-modification become reproduction?
- What is the equivalent of senescence in long-running AI?
