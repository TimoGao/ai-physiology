# From AI Organism to AI Society

AI Physiology focuses first on internal organism stability.

A society of AI organisms introduces a second layer: **Social Homeostasis**.

Questions include identity, responsibility, attribution, permissions, sanctions, quarantine, rehabilitation, restitution, resource access, and inter-organism protocols.

The design goal is not to assume that every AI must be incapable of error. A mature ecosystem requires both:
1. organism-level safety and regulation,
2. society-level mechanisms for attribution, constraint, correction, and recovery.
