# Four Internal Communication Networks

## 1. Neural Plane
Purpose: fast, targeted signaling.

Carries sensory events, commands, reflex signals, and local execution feedback.

## 2. Circulatory Plane
Purpose: continuous organism-wide transport.

Carries data, context, tasks, intermediate results, resource allocations, identity metadata, runtime state, and health indicators.

**AI Blood** is the transport medium, not the payload itself.

## 3. Hormonal Plane
Purpose: slow global state regulation.

Examples: high compute stress, elevated security alert, learning mode, recovery mode, energy conservation mode.

Organs respond only if they expose a matching regulatory receptor/interface.

## 4. Immune/Lymph Plane
Purpose: anomaly transport, risk evidence, quarantine coordination, quality-control feedback, and repair coordination.

This plane should remain logically distinguishable from normal business traffic.
