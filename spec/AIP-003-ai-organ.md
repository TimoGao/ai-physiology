# AIP-003: AI Organ

**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1

## Definition

An **AI Organ** is a specialized subsystem composed of one or more AI Cells or tissues that performs a stable physiological role for the organism.

## Organ qualification criteria

A subsystem SHOULD satisfy most of the following:
1. specialized responsibility
2. explicit boundary
3. standardized inputs/outputs
4. local autonomy
5. independent health state
6. replaceability or repairability
7. contribution to organism-level homeostasis

A feature, skill, prompt, or library is not automatically an organ.
