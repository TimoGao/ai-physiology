# AIP-001: AI Organism

**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1

## Definition

An **AI Organism** is a bounded, persistent, autonomous or semi-autonomous artificial system that coordinates multiple specialized subsystems to sense, regulate, maintain, repair, and act while preserving organizational continuity over time.

## Minimum properties

An AI Organism SHOULD exhibit:
- identifiable boundary
- persistent identity
- internal state
- multi-subsystem organization
- resource consumption
- internal communication
- local autonomous control
- feedback regulation
- failure handling
- continuity across component replacement

## Non-example

A stateless single inference request to a model is not, by itself, an AI Organism.
